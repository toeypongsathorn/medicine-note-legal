Delete your Medicine Note account

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Medicine Note account and the data that goes with it. It applies to the iOS and Android apps, operated by Makesense Solution.

Option 1: Delete it in the app

  1. Open Medicine Note and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when the app asks you.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you cannot open the app, send an email to pongsathorn_c@yahoo.co.th with the subject "Delete my Medicine Note account". Write from the email address you used to sign up. If you signed up with Google, Facebook or Apple, tell us which one and the email address linked to it. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We will reply by email to confirm your request. The 30-day deletion period below then starts.

What happens next

  • Your account and all data linked to it are permanently deleted 30 days after your request.
  • During those 30 days you can cancel the deletion. In the app, sign in again and choose to keep your account.
  • Deleted from our servers: your account details, settings, the people you added, onboarding answers, subscription feedback, and any medication records and photos that were synced while you had Premium.
  • We may keep a small amount of data for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons, as the Privacy Policy explains.

Data on your phone and in backups

  • On the Free plan, your medication records are stored only on your phone. We do not have them. Remove them by deleting the app or by clearing its storage in your phone settings.
  • A copy may remain in your own device backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iOS). Delete that backup in your Google or Apple account settings if you want it gone.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it yourself. On Android, open the Google Play app, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. On iOS, open iPhone Settings, then your Apple ID, then Subscriptions.
