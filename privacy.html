Privacy Policy for Medicine Note

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Makesense Solution ("we", "us") operates Medicine Note (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address (or, if you sign in with Google, Facebook or Apple, the name and email those providers share with us). It is used only to authenticate you and identify your data.

Health and protocol data. The App records health-related information you choose to enter: medication and supplement names, doses and schedules; injection-site history; inventory levels; side-effect and health-diary logs; lab results; body weight, body fat, blood pressure and pulse; and photos you attach (of medications, restocks, or lab reports).

Apple Health data. If you enable Health sync in Settings, the App reads body weight, body fat percentage and workout data from Apple Health, with your explicit permission through iOS's HealthKit prompt. You can revoke this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health. The App does not write data to Apple Health, and Health data is never used for advertising.

Device identifier. The App generates a random identifier stored in your device's Keychain, used only to enforce the free-tier one-device limit. It is not used for advertising.

Purchase information. If you subscribe to Premium, purchases are handled by Apple and our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We receive only your subscription status. We never receive your payment card details.

Advertising data (Free tier). Free accounts see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general usage and device information to serve and measure ads. If you are in a region that requires consent, you are asked for it through Google's consent prompt, and on iOS you are asked for App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking. You may decline; you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. Your medication, health and lab data are never shared with advertisers. Premium accounts do not see ads.

2. How your data is stored

Free tier: your protocol data is stored only on your device. We do not have access to it and it is not transmitted to our servers.

Premium tier: your protocol data, including photos, is synced to our servers (hosted on Supabase) so it is available on every device you sign into. Access is restricted to your own account through database row-level security. Our own access is limited to what is needed to operate and support the App.

3. How we use your data

We use your data only to operate the App: to save and sync your protocol, authenticate you, enforce the free-tier device limit, process your subscription, and show ads on the Free tier. We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers.

4. Third parties

  • Supabase – authentication and Premium data storage
  • RevenueCat – subscription and purchase management
  • Google AdMob – advertising on Free accounts
  • Apple, Google, Facebook – sign-in providers, if you choose to use them

Each processes data only as needed to provide its service. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs.

5. Your choices

  • You can export your data at any time from Settings.
  • You can revoke Apple Health access in iOS Settings.
  • You can cancel Premium in Settings > Manage Subscription, which opens Apple's subscription management screen.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier or turn off tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us below.

6. Data retention

We keep account data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your associated data from our servers, except what we must retain for legal, tax or fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date and, where required, by notice in the App.
