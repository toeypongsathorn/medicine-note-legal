Privacy Policy for Medicine Note

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Makesense Solution ("we", "us") operates Medicine Note (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address (or, if you sign in with Google, Facebook or Apple, the name and email those providers share with us). It is used only to authenticate you and identify your data.

Onboarding information. When you set up the App you answer questions: for example whether you use it for yourself or for others, how you heard about it, the kinds and number of medications you take, how often you miss doses, your daily routine, your sex, date of birth, weight, height and goals, and your theme, sound and permission choices, as well as what you choose on the subscription screen. We store these answers on our servers with your account, whether or not you subscribe, to set up the App and to understand how people use it so we can improve it. They are never shared with advertisers.

People you add. You can add other people, such as a child, a parent or a pet, and track their medications. We store the names, avatar emoji and photos you enter for them as part of your account settings, together with the medication records you create for them. Add information about another person only if you are entitled to. It is used only to provide the App and is not visible to other users.

Health and protocol data. The App records health-related information you choose to enter: medication and supplement names, doses and schedules; injection-site history; inventory levels; side-effect and health-diary logs; lab results; body weight, body fat, blood pressure and pulse; and photos you attach (of medications, restocks, or lab reports).

Apple Health data. If you enable Health sync in Settings, the App reads body weight, body fat percentage and blood pressure from Apple Health, with your explicit permission through iOS's HealthKit prompt. You can revoke this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health. The App does not write data to Apple Health, and Health data is never used for advertising.

Device identifier. The App generates a random identifier stored in your device's Keychain, used only to enforce the free-tier one-device limit. It is not used for advertising.

Subscription feedback. If you cancel Premium from the App you may choose a reason and write a comment. If you do, we store it with your account to understand why people leave.

Purchase information. If you subscribe to Premium, purchases are handled by Apple and our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We receive only your subscription status. We never receive your payment card details.

Advertising data (Free plan). Accounts without an active subscription (the Free plan) see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general usage and device information to serve and measure ads. If you are in a region that requires consent, you are asked for it through Google's consent prompt, and on iOS you are asked for App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking. You may decline; you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. Your medication, health and lab data are never shared with advertisers. Premium accounts do not see ads.

2. How your data is stored

Free plan: your medication, dose, lab, diary and body-metric records, and the photos you attach, are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. For every account, free or Premium, we store on our servers your account details, your app settings (including your height and the people you add) and your onboarding answers.

Premium tier: your protocol data, including photos, is synced to our servers (hosted on Supabase) so it is available on every device you sign into. Access is restricted to your own account through database row-level security. Our own access is limited to what is needed to operate and support the App.

If your subscription ends: your account moves to the Free plan and you keep using the copy of your data that is on your device. The records and photos that were synced while you were Premium remain on our servers, and are not synced further, until you delete your account.

3. How we use your data

We use your data only to operate the App: to save and sync your protocol, authenticate you, enforce the free-tier device limit, process your subscription, show ads on the Free plan, and understand how people use the App so we can improve it (using the onboarding answers described above). We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers.

4. Third parties

  • Supabase – authentication and Premium data storage
  • RevenueCat – subscription and purchase management
  • Google AdMob – advertising on the Free plan
  • Apple, Google, Facebook – sign-in providers, if you choose to use them

Each processes data only as needed to provide its service. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs. The onboarding answers described above are our own first-party analytics, stored on Supabase.

5. Your choices

  • You can export your data at any time from Settings.
  • You can revoke Apple Health access in iOS Settings.
  • You can cancel Premium in Settings > Manage Subscription, which opens Apple's subscription management screen.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier or turn off tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • You can delete your account in the App under Settings > Account > Delete Account. Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted 30 days after you request it; if you sign back in during those 30 days you can cancel the deletion. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription — cancel it in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

6. Data retention

We keep account data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we permanently delete your associated data from our servers 30 days after your request, except what we must retain for legal, tax or fraud-prevention purposes. Your onboarding answers and subscription feedback are deleted with your account. Data stored only on your own device (Free plan) stays on the device until you remove the App.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The App is for adults: you may enter information about a child you care for, but the account holder must be an adult.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date and, where required, by notice in the App.
